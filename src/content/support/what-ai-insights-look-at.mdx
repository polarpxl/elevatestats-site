AI Insights are short, data-driven coaching takeaways generated after every finalized game. Three to four cards appear on your home dashboard, each grounded in your team's actual game data. AI Insights are a PRO feature.

## How it works

When you finalize a game, the app sends a summary of your team's stats to an AI model and gets back three to four short insights. The summary includes your season record, goals for and against, your last 5 games, your top scorers, and the box score from the game you just finalized.

Insights fall into one of four types:

- **Performance** - what's working well right now
- **Trend** - patterns building over multiple games
- **Player highlight** - individual players standing out
- **Concern** - areas that need attention

Each insight references specific numbers from your data, like goals per game, scoring streaks, or shot totals. They're written in a coaching tone, not a marketing tone, and they're meant to be the kind of observation an assistant coach might raise at the next practice.

Here's an example of what an insight looks like:

> The team has struggled offensively in the last 5 games, averaging just 1.4 goals per game compared to the season average of 4.1, with three scoreless periods including back-to-back 0-0 ties.

## Where to find them

AI Insights appear on your home dashboard, between your season record and your recent games. The carousel is swipeable, with pagination dots showing how many insights are in the current batch. A label below the carousel tells you which game the insights were generated after.

Insights stay visible until your next finalized game replaces them with a fresh batch.

## Step-by-step

1. Finalize a game from inside the live tracking screen.
2. The app generates insights automatically. This usually takes a few seconds.
3. Open your home dashboard.
4. Swipe through the cards to read each insight.
