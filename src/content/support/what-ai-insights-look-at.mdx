AI Insights is a swipeable carousel that shows up after a game is finalized. It reads the tracked events from that game, compares them to your team trend, and surfaces three to five short takeaways you might actually talk about at practice.

## How it works

The model looks at shot location clusters, shooting efficiency shifts, goalie workload, line combinations that produced the most chances, and opponent tendencies. It does not invent data. Every claim it makes points back to specific events in the game so you can verify it.

## Step-by-step

1. Finalize a game.
2. Wait a few seconds while the insights generate.
3. Swipe through the cards.
4. Tap any card to jump into the game detail page at the events that back it up.
