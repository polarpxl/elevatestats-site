You can upgrade to PRO anytime from inside the app. Billing is handled by Stripe, and your data stays with you whether you upgrade now, later, or come back after a break.

## How it works

PRO is $14 CAD per month or $109 CAD per year. The yearly plan works out to $9.08 per month and saves you $59 a year. You can cancel anytime. When you cancel, you keep PRO features until the end of your billing period, then drop back to Free with all your data intact.

If you upgrade during your 3-game PRO trial, the remaining trial games no longer matter. You're a PRO subscriber from that point on, with full access to every feature.

If you've been on PRO before, cancelled, and are coming back, your historical data is still there. Upgrading again restores everything you had, including past AI Insights and full game history.

## Step-by-step

1. Open the app and tap Settings.
2. Under Billing, tap Upgrade to PRO.
3. Choose monthly or yearly.
4. Enter your payment details on the Stripe checkout screen.
5. Tap the subscribe button at the bottom.
6. You'll be taken back to your dashboard as a PRO subscriber. Stripe will email you a receipt.
