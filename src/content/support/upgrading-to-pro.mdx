You can upgrade to PRO anytime from inside the app. Billing is handled by Stripe, and you can switch between monthly and yearly whenever you want.

## How it works

PRO is $14 CAD per month or $140 CAD per year (save 17% annually). Your free forever plan does not go away when you upgrade. If you cancel, you keep PRO features until the end of the billing period and then drop back to Free with everything you had before still intact.

## Step-by-step

1. Open Settings and tap Plan.
2. Choose PRO, then pick monthly or yearly.
3. Enter your payment details.
4. PRO features unlock immediately.
