Rinks have terrible signal. Elevate is built to assume that. Every event you log lands in an on-device queue first, then syncs to the cloud in the background when the phone can reach it.

## How it works

Before a game starts, Elevate prefetches your roster, the opponent card, and the game schedule. Once those are cached, you can put the phone in airplane mode and the tracking screen still works exactly the same. When you come back online, the queue flushes automatically. Every event has a unique ID, so reconnecting mid-sync does not double-count anything.

## Step-by-step

1. Open the game on the rink before you drop off the network.
2. Track normally. The cloud icon in the corner tells you if you are online.
3. Finalize the game when it is over. If sync has not finished, the app keeps the queue until it does.
4. Open the app later, on WiFi or cellular, to see the finalized stats and AI Insights.
