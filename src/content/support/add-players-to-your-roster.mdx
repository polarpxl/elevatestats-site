Once your team exists, the next job is the roster. You can add players one at a time, or bulk-upload them via CSV if you are migrating from a spreadsheet.

## How it works

Each player has a name, a jersey number, a position, and a handedness. You can mark a player as the starting goalie, and you can toggle players on or off the active lineup for a specific game without losing them from the roster.

## Step-by-step

1. Open your team.
2. Tap Roster, then Add player.
3. Fill in the basics and save.
4. Repeat for every player on the sheet.
5. If you have a CSV, tap Import instead and follow the prompts.
