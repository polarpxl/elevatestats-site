The shot map plots every shot event (goal, shot on net, miss, block) at the spot on the rink where you logged it. Colour tells you the outcome, and size tells you the frequency when dots overlap.

## How it works

Each dot is a single event. Goals are filled orange, shots on net are filled blue, missed shots are hollow, blocked shots have a diagonal slash. You can filter by player, by game, or by game type. Team shot maps show everything for the team across the filter.

## Step-by-step

1. Open a team, player, or game detail page.
2. Tap the Shot map tile.
3. Use the filters at the top to narrow by player, date range, or opponent.
4. Tap any dot to see the game, time, and player attached to that event.
