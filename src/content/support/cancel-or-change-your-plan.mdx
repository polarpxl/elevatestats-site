You can cancel PRO, switch between monthly and yearly, or come back months later without losing any data. Everything you tracked stays on your account.

## How it works

Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period. You keep PRO features until then. After that, your account drops back to Free. Your games, rosters, and history are all still there. If you come back, upgrading again restores full PRO access instantly.

## Step-by-step

1. Open Settings and tap Plan.
2. Tap Change plan or Cancel.
3. Confirm the change. You will see the new billing date on the confirmation screen.
