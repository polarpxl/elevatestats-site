You can cancel PRO, switch between monthly and yearly, or come back months later without losing any of your data. Everything you tracked stays on your account.

## How it works

Cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period. You keep PRO features until then, and your account drops back to Free after that. Your games, rosters, history, and player attribution all stay in your account. If you upgrade again later, full PRO access restores instantly, including past AI Insights and full game history.

The 3-game PRO trial is a one-time benefit for new accounts. Once you've upgraded to PRO and then cancelled, you don't get the trial again. You drop back to Free with the standard Free tier features.

To switch between monthly and yearly, you can update your plan directly in Stripe without losing access. Switching takes effect at your next billing cycle.

## Step-by-step

1. Open the app and tap Settings.
2. Under Billing, tap Manage subscription. This takes you to the Stripe billing portal.
3. To cancel, tap Cancel subscription and confirm. You'll keep PRO until the end of your current billing period, then drop back to Free.
4. To switch between monthly and yearly, tap Update plan and choose the new option. The change applies on your next billing date.
5. To update your payment method or view invoices, use the relevant section in the Stripe portal.
