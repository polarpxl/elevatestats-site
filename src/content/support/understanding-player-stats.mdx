Each player has a profile page with a set of stats you would expect to see on a real hockey card. Elevate keeps the definitions simple and the math honest.

## How it works

Player pages show goals, assists, shots, shooting percentage, and a plus-minus figure that only counts the events you actually tracked. If a game was tracked in Quick mode, plus-minus is not available for that game because the app did not capture per-player presence on the ice.

## Step-by-step

1. Open your team.
2. Tap Roster, then pick a player.
3. Scroll through the stat blocks: Season, Last 5, and per-game splits.
4. Tap any stat tile to see the underlying per-game breakdown.
