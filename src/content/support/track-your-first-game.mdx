The tracking screen is built for one-handed use on the bench. Big buttons, zero menus in the way, and every tap is logged instantly on-device before any network call.

<Callout type="info">Working offline? Tap the cloud icon to check sync status.</Callout>

## How it works

When you start a game, Elevate pre-loads your roster, the opponent, and any pre-game notes so nothing is missing if signal drops. Events are logged to the rink visual by tapping where the event happened. Goals, shots on net, missed shots, and blocked shots all take one tap, plus a quick player attribution.

<Screenshot src="/support/placeholder-screenshot.svg" alt="Game tracking screen" caption="The main tracking screen during a live game." width={1600} height={900} />

## Step-by-step

1. From your team, tap Start game.
2. Pick the opponent, set the period length, and confirm the starting goalie.
3. Tap the rink where the event happened, then choose the event type.
4. Assign the player (and any assists, if it is a goal).
5. Tap Finalize when the game is over to lock in stats and trigger AI Insights.
