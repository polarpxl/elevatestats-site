Tracking a game with Elevate is built for one-handed use on the bench. Big buttons, zero menus in the way, every tap logged instantly on-device.

<Callout type="info">No signal at the rink? Elevate works fully offline. See [Working offline and how sync works](/support/working-offline-and-how-sync-works).</Callout>

## How it works

When you start a game, Elevate loads your roster, the opponent, your goalie, and your period lengths so you're ready to go. Once the puck drops, you tap anywhere on the rink to log an event. A tray slides up asking which team the event belongs to, and PRO subscribers can also select the player involved, including assists on goals.

On Free, every event is attributed to your team or the opponent. PRO unlocks per-player attribution, so every goal, assist, and shot ties back to a specific player on your roster. Goals also ask who was on the ice, which is how plus-minus is counted. See [Plus-minus and who was on the ice](/support/plus-minus-and-who-was-on-the-ice).

Throughout the game, you can toggle power play on or off for either team, advance the period as the game moves from first to second to third, undo any event by tapping the undo button at the top of the screen (tap multiple times to undo further back), and view live stats at the bottom of the screen.

## Step-by-step

1. From your dashboard, tap Track.
2. If you've already scheduled the game, select it from the list. Otherwise, tap to create a game and set the date, game type, home or away, opponent, starting goalie, and period length.
3. Tap Start Game to begin tracking.
4. Tap the rink where the event happened. The tray slides up.
5. Choose your team or the opponent. On PRO, tap the player: shots, misses and blocks save on that tap. Goals ask for the scorer, assists and who was on the ice.
6. Toggle the PP indicator if the event happened on a power play.
7. Advance the period at the top of the screen as the game moves forward.
8. When the game is over, tap the settings icon at the top right to open Live Game Settings, tap **End Game**, then confirm. Final stats lock in and AI Insights generate for PRO subscribers. Ending a game needs a connection.
