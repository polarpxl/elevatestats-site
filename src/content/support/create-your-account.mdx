Creating an account takes about a minute. You can sign up with email and password, or with Google. Either way, you start with a 3-game PRO trial. No credit card required.

## Where to sign up

Tap **Start free** anywhere on the marketing site, or head straight to the app at [app.elevatestats.app](https://app.elevatestats.app) and tap Sign up.

## Sign up with email

1. Enter your name, email, and a password (minimum 8 characters).
2. Tap **Sign Up**.
3. The app shows a "Check your email" screen. Open your inbox and look for the confirmation email from Elevate.
4. Tap the link in the email to confirm your account.
5. You're sent to your dashboard.

If the email doesn't show up after a minute, check your spam folder. You can also tap **Didn't get it? Resend email** on the confirmation screen to send another one. If you typed the wrong email, tap **Wrong email? Go back** to start over.

## Sign up with Google

1. On the Sign up screen, tap **Continue with Google**.
2. Pick your Google account and confirm.
3. You're sent straight to your dashboard. No email confirmation needed.

## What happens next

Your dashboard greets you with a banner that says "No team yet. Go to Manage to create your first team." That's your first step.

Once your team is created, the trial counter appears at the top of your dashboard showing **1 of 3 games left for PRO**. You're now ready to set up your roster and track your first game.

For the next steps, see [Set up your first team](/support/set-up-your-first-team) and [Add players to your roster](/support/add-players-to-your-roster).
