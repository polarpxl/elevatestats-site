To create your account, open the app on your phone and tap Sign up. You only need an email address and a password to get started. No credit card, no phone verification.

## How it works

Your account is tied to the email you sign up with. That is where game reports, receipts, and the occasional product update will go. You can change it later under Settings.

## Step-by-step

1. Open the app and tap Sign up.
2. Enter your email address and pick a password.
3. Confirm your email by tapping the link we send you.
4. Sign in and add your first team.
