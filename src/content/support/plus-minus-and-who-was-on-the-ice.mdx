With full tracking (Quick mode off), every goal that counts for plus-minus asks one more thing: who was on the ice. That's how Elevate counts plus-minus (+/-). Shots, misses and blocks don't change, and they never ask who was on.

Plus-minus is a PRO feature.

## How plus-minus is counted

Elevate follows the NHL rules:

- **Even strength goal for** - every skater you had on the ice gets +1.
- **Even strength goal against** - every skater you had on the ice gets -1.
- **Shorthanded goals** count the same way: +1 when you score one, -1 when you give one up.
- **Power play goals** don't count for plus-minus, for either team.
- **Goalies** never get plus-minus.

Whether a goal counts depends on the power play switch at the moment of the goal, so keep the PP switch up to date.

## Record a goal for your team

The goal sheet shows your whole roster. A bar above it says what your next tap records: **Goal**, **Assist** or **Plus**. It moves along by itself.

1. Tap the rink where the goal happened, then tap **Goal**.
2. Tap the scorer. The bar moves to Assist.
3. Tap up to two assists. After the second one, the bar moves to Plus.
4. Tap the other players who were on the ice. With one assist or none, tap **Plus** in the bar first.
5. Tap **Confirm goal**.

The scorer and assists count as on the ice. You need at least 3 on the ice and can have up to 6 (6 means the goalie was pulled). Tapped the wrong player? Tap them again to clear them.

## Record a goal against

1. Tap the rink, then **Goal**, and pick the opponent.
2. Tap the players who were on the ice. They get a minus.
3. Tap **Confirm goal against**.

You need at least 3 on the ice.

## Power play goals

Power play goals don't count for plus-minus, so the sheet doesn't ask who was on. Your power play goal: tap the scorer and assists, then confirm. Their power play goal: just confirm.

Shorthanded goals do count, so they still ask.

## Where plus-minus shows up

- **Player page** - +/- in the top stats strip and in the Seasons table.
- **Game detail** - a +/- column in the skaters table.
- **Analyze** - a +/- tab in Roster Analysis to sort your skaters by it.
- **CSV export** - a plus_minus column in the player stats export.

Plus-minus appears once you've recorded who was on the ice for at least one goal. Games tracked before plus-minus, or in Quick mode, don't count toward it.

## A few things worth knowing

- Not sure who was on? Your best guess is fine. It doesn't change the score or anyone's goals and assists.
- Quick mode skips this step, so games tracked in Quick mode have no plus-minus. Free teams track in Quick mode.
- Once a goal is saved, who was on the ice can't be changed yet.
