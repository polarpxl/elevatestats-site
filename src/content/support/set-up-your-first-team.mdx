Your team is the container for everything else you track. Give it a name, pick a level and league, and Elevate will set up the rest.

## How it works

Every game, roster, and stat is linked to a team. You can run as many teams as you need (U9 house league and your U15 AAA side can live on the same account), and switching between them is one tap in the top menu.

## Step-by-step

1. From the home screen, tap Add team.
2. Enter the team name, level, league, and season.
3. Pick a team colour to make the team card easy to spot.
4. Save. You can start adding players right away, or come back to it later.
