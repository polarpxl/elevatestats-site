Your team is the container for everything else you track. Once it's set up, you can start adding players and opponents.

## How it works

After you create your account, your dashboard will be empty with a message telling you to head to Manage to create your first team. From the Manage screen, tap Create team to open the setup modal.

The team abbreviation matters more than it looks. It's used throughout the app, including the live tracking screen during games. Stick to something short and recognizable, like STO for Stoney Creek or COL for Collingwood.

## Step-by-step

1. From your dashboard or the Manage screen, tap Create team.
2. Fill in the team name (for example, Stoney Creek Stars).
3. Set the team abbreviation. Up to four letters, all caps (for example, STO).
4. Enter the level (for example, U12 AA).
5. Enter the league.
6. Set the Season.
7. Tap Create team. You're taken to the Manage screen, where you can add your roster and opponents next.
