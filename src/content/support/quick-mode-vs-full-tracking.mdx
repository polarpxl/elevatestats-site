Elevate has two tracking depths. Quick mode is team-vs-opponent only — a fast way to log shots and goals without pausing to pick players. Full tracking adds player attribution for every event.

## How it works

Quick mode is the default on the free plan. You tap to log the event and the team it belongs to, and that is it. Full tracking asks for the scorer, the assists, and any other involved players from your roster.

## Step-by-step

1. Before the game starts, open Settings inside the game screen.
2. Choose Quick mode or Full tracking.
3. Stick with one for the whole game so the stats stay consistent.
