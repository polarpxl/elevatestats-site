Elevate has two plans. Free is free forever. PRO is a monthly or yearly subscription that unlocks the analytics and insights layer.

## How it works

On Free, you get unlimited game tracking in Quick mode, team scores and shots, and a running season record. You also get three free PRO-level games to try the full tracking depth, AI Insights, and full player pages.

PRO unlocks everything full-time: per-player stats, goalie stats, shot maps, AI Coaching Insights, opponent analysis, full game history, and CSV import and export.

## Step-by-step

1. Open Settings inside the app.
2. Tap Plan.
3. Compare the two plans side by side.
4. When you are ready, upgrade. You can cancel anytime.
